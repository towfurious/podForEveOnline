## Full description (~1450/4000 chars)
```
PodForEve is an unofficial companion app for EVE Online — check your character's skill training, wallet, planetary interaction, and industry jobs anywhere, without opening the client.

FEATURES

• Skill Queue — see your full training queue, current skill progress, and time remaining at a glance.

• Wallet — recent ISK transactions (bounties, market trades, PI taxes, and more) with a running balance.

• Planetary Interaction — every colony's extractor countdown, factory status, and storage fill levels in one view, with alerts before extraction stops.

• Industry Jobs — track manufacturing, research, and invention jobs from start to delivery.

• Notifications — get notified when a skill finishes training or an industry job completes, even if the app isn't open.

• Try it without an account — Demo Mode lets you explore every screen with sample data before signing in with your own EVE Online account.

• Offline-friendly — your last-synced data stays available even without a connection.

• Five color themes — including faction-inspired looks and a full AMOLED black theme.

HOW IT WORKS

PodForEve signs you in through EVE Online's own official login (CCP's SSO) — your password is never seen or stored by this app. Your character data is cached only on your device; PodForEve has no server of its own.

EVE Online is a trademark of CCP hf. PodForEve is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by CCP hf.
```
